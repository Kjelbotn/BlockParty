# BlockParty 0.3.2-alpha

## Gameplay and presentation
- Increased the default dance floor to 80×80; supported custom sizes are 40–96.
- Expanded the spectator route and seating tiers.
- Rebuilt the block-letter target display as a double-sided board and corrected the two viewing orientations.
- Repeated the action-bar colour cue during preparation so short rounds keep the target visible above the hotbar.
- Kept all dance-floor tiles as concrete; target items are concrete as well.
- Kept random pattern generation but removed pattern announcements from player-facing messages.
- Sidebar scoreboard displays dancers left, round, time, points and power-ups.
- Elimination message is `<player> tapte konkurransen!`; visual-only lightning now strikes at the falling player's position.
- Power-up beacon appears in the air one block above the floor tile and is restored cleanly when collected or when the round ends.
- Power-up effects are removed at the end of their current round.

## Audio
- Replaced previous music loops and sound effects with original, softer electronic tracks and more restrained one-shot cues.
- Kept music synchronized to round start and round transitions.

## Compatibility
- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.x, Java 21.
- Singleplayer is the primary target; solo play supports all 15 rounds.
