# BlockParty 0.3.0-alpha

- Expanded the generated arena around a 40x40 default concrete dance floor.
- Added a continuous all-sides spectator promenade and stepped viewing stands.
- Added a block-built target-colour display, with display text oriented to face into the arena.
- Corrected the lobby BP logo lettering orientation and reading order.
- Added three original music cues, seven original sound effects, and Norwegian/English subtitles.
- Music starts with each round and stops at round resolution/intermission.
- Added a random beacon power-up chance on selected longer rounds.
- Added Speed II, Night Vision and Jump Boost II; effects are removed at the end of the current round.
- No Second Chance power-up and no music voting in this version.
