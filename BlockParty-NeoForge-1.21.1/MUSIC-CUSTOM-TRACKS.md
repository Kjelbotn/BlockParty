# BlockParty official music playlist

The official BlockParty playlist contains four songs:

1. **BlockParty** — Bangerfabrikken (`bangerfabrikken.ogg`)
2. **Odin er en alkis - Moon Edition** — Bangerfabrikken (`odin_moon.ogg`)
3. **Stille Anders [Indiepop]** — Bangerfabrikken (`stille_anders.ogg`)
4. **Hjulene på bussen (til Olli)** — Bangerfabrikken (`hjulene_pa_bussen_olli.ogg`)

The two newly supplied tracks are included as streamed OGG resources and are available in the lobby voting playlist.
Existing audio files are intentionally not replaced or re-encoded.
