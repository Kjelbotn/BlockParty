# BlockParty official music playlist

The official BlockParty playlist contains exactly two songs:

1. **BlockParty** — Bangerfabrikken (`bangerfabrikken.ogg`)
2. **Odin er en alkis - Moon Edition** — Bangerfabrikken (`odin_moon.ogg`)

The former Neon Beat, Pixel Rush, Night Drive and empty custom slots were development/test tracks and are no longer included in the voting menu or mod resources.
